Personalities

People who inspire me — their ideas, their lives, what they teach me.

RF

Richard Feynman

Physicist · 1918–1988

He made physics feel like play. His lectures still feel alive decades later. What I love most: he never pretended to understand something he didn't. "I would rather have questions that can't be answered than answers that can't be questioned."

Why he inspires me: his curiosity was never performative. It was genuine, childlike, relentless.

DR

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

Jurist, Economist, Social Reformer · 1891–1956

The most educated Indian of his time, and the most ignored in his lifetime. He believed education was the only real liberation. He read more than almost anyone I know of — his personal library had over 50,000 books.

Why he inspires me: he never let the world's opinion of him limit his opinion of himself.
