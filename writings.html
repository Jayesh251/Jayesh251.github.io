The old man and the chai shop

Apr 20, 2026Short Story

Every morning at 7am, there is an old man at the corner chai shop near my building. He always sits at the same table, alone, with two cups. One for him. One, I assumed, for someone who never comes.

One day I asked the chai wala about him. "His wife used to come with him every day for thirty years," he said. "She passed two years ago. He still orders two cups."

I don't know his name. But I think about him more than I think about most people I know.
