Finally understood Docker

May 8, 2026Work

I've used Docker for months but never really understood it. This week it clicked. The key insight: a container is just a process with a restricted view of the system. It's not a virtual machine. It's not magic. It's isolation.

What I actually learned:

  • Difference between an image and a container
  • How volumes persist data outside the container lifecycle
  • Why docker-compose makes multi-service setups manageable
  • The mental model: containers are cattle, not pets

What I built: A 3-container app — Python API, Postgres DB, nginx proxy — all connected with docker-compose. It worked first try (after 6 failed tries).

What I still want to learn: Kubernetes. Someday. Not today.

Git rebase vs merge — it finally makes sense

May 3, 2026Work

Merge keeps history as-is, with a merge commit. Rebase rewrites history to look linear. Neither is wrong — it depends on what story you want your git log to tell.

Our team uses rebase for feature branches, merge for releases. Now that I understand why, I've stopped being scared of it.
