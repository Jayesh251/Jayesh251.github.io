Day in Life

Small moments. Days that meant something. What I did, what I felt.

I met a street dog today

May 10, 2026

He was sitting outside the gate of my building. Brown, medium-sized, calm eyes. I sat down on the steps and he walked over and just... sat next to me. No food involved. No tricks. He just wanted to be near someone.

We sat there for almost an hour. I was supposed to be going back upstairs to work. I didn't. Some things are more important than work.

I don't know his name. I gave him some biscuits before leaving. He didn't follow me — he just watched me go, then turned back to the gate like he had somewhere important to be.

I felt something like peace that evening. The kind you don't plan for.

Dinner with the team

May 8, 2026

We went to that old restaurant near the office — the one with the terrible lighting and incredible dal makhani. Seven of us. We talked for three hours.

About work, about life, about nothing. About Rohit's terrible jokes. About Priya's upcoming move. About why none of us sleep enough.

I think I forget sometimes how lucky I am to like the people I work with. Today I remembered.
